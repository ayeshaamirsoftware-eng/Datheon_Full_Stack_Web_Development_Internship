StudyCore / INTERACTIVE CASE STUDY
02 / LEARN WITH EVIDENCE

StudyCoreYour notes. Your answers.

A study companion that turns your own files and pasted notes into answers you can trace, flashcards you can revisit, and quizzes that help you put your understanding to the test. Organised by subject, grounded in your material.

FastAPIPostgreSQLHybrid retrievalSource validation
Open StudyCore ↗Source code ↗
Inside the application

A React interface and FastAPI backend connect subject-based notes to page-aware processing, semantic and keyword search, and source-linked answers. Original notes stay in private Supabase storage. The semantic model is packaged with the deployment so study sessions do not depend on a laptop or a fresh model download.
